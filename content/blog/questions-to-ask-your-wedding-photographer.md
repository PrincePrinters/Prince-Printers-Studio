Shaadi ek baar hoti hai, aur uski photos zindagi bhar saath rehti hain. Booking se pehle yeh sawal poochna na bhooliye.

## 1. Kya main aapki poori wedding gallery dekh sakta/sakti hoon?

Instagram par sirf best 10 photos hoti hain. Ek poori shaadi ki gallery dekhiye — usse pata chalega ki photographer har ritual aur har family member ko kaise cover karta hai.

## 2. Shoot par kaun aayega?

Kai studios mein owner khud nahi aate. Pooch lijiye ki aapki shaadi par kaun-kaun photographer aur videographer honge.

## 3. Photos aur film kitne din mein milenge?

Preview photos, poori gallery, highlight film aur album — har cheez ki delivery date likhit mein lijiye.

## 4. Guests ko photos kaise milengi?

Kya guests QR code ya event code se apni photos khud download kar sakte hain? Isse aapko baad mein sabko photos bhejne ki tension nahi rahegi.

## 5. Package mein exactly kya-kya shaamil hai?

Kitne din, kitne photographers, drone, album ke pages, raw files — sab kuch quotation mein likha hona chahiye.

## 6. Advance aur cancellation ka niyam kya hai?

Date badalne ya cancel hone par kya hota hai, yeh pehle se saaf kar lijiye.

## 7. Backup ka kya intezaam hai?

Achhe studios do memory cards par record karte hain aur files ka cloud backup rakhte hain. Zaroor poochiye.

Koi aur sawal ho to humse seedha baat kariye — hum har sawal ka saaf jawab denge.
