Shaadi ke baad sabse common sawal hota hai — *"meri photos kab milengi?"* Ab iska jawab bahut aasaan hai.

## Step 1: Event kholiye

Do tarike hain:

1. **QR code scan kariye** — venue par ya invitation par laga QR apne phone ke camera se scan kariye. Event ki gallery seedha khul jaayegi.
2. **Event code daaliye** — website ke home page par "Event Code" box mein code likhiye (jaise `RAHUL2026`) aur **Open Photos** dabaiye.

## Step 2: "Find My Photos" se selfie lijiye

Gallery mein **Find My Photos** par tap kariye aur ek selfie lijiye (ya gallery se apni koi saaf photo chuniye).

- Chehra saamne se aur achhi roshni mein ho.
- Chashma ya mask ho to hata kar selfie lein — result behtar aata hai.

Aapki selfie **aapke phone se bahar nahi jaati** — matching aapke phone ke browser mein hi hoti hai.

## Step 3: Download kariye

- Kisi bhi photo par tap kariye aur **Download** dabaiye.
- Ya **"Meri Saari Photos Download"** dabakar ek saath sab le lijiye.

Agar selfie se koi photo na mile, to **"Saari Photos Dikhaayein"** dabakar poori gallery dekh sakte hain.

## Koi dikkat?

Event code yaad nahi ya gallery nahi khul rahi — studio ko WhatsApp kariye, hum turant madad karenge.
